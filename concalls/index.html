How to read this

Tone = 100 × (positive − negative) ÷ (positive + negative) words, counted only in what management said (moderator and analyst turns removed by speaker labels). A word right after not / no / never / without (within 3 words) counts for the other side.

Calls with fewer than 15 scored words get no tone. It is a word count, not a judgement: "lower costs" counts as negative. Compare a company with its own earlier calls more than with other companies.

How to read this

One card per company per quarter. Links go to the filings on NSE: the transcript PDF, the recording intimation, and the audio/video link printed inside that intimation when one was found.

Key numbers are sentences by management that contain a forward-looking word (expect, guidance, target, plan to, aim, outlook, we will…), a metric (revenue, EBITDA margin, order inflow, capex, loan growth…) and a value (a %, a ₹ Cr amount, bps, or words like "mid-teens" = 14–16%). Numbers about the industry, the market or the Union Budget are dropped, as are statements about periods already over. Tap a line for the full quote and page.

Transcripts are parsed for companies of ₹1,000 Cr market cap and above; smaller companies list their links only. "Transcript queued" means the PDF is on NSE but has not been read yet (the nightly run reads more each night).

How these lists are made

Raised / cut: the same company, metric, period and unit (e.g. FY27 revenue growth in %) stated on two consecutive calls; the median value on the newer call is compared with the older one. A change of at least 1 point (0.5 point for margins, 25 bps, or 5% for ₹ amounts) counts. Higher credit-cost guidance counts as a cut.

Big capex: capex or investment amounts in ₹ Cr stated with a plan/target on the latest two quarters' calls, at least ₹500 Cr or 5% of market cap, ranked by amount ÷ market cap. Amounts above 4× market cap are dropped as likely industry figures.

Guidance ledger

Metric Call
How to read this

Every guidance statement found in the transcripts, as metric · period · value, with the verbatim sentence and the transcript page.

Status compares the statement with reported quarterly results (NSE XBRL filings, via the Earnings Centre store): revenue / PAT / EBITDA growth = the period's total vs the same quarters a year earlier; margins = the period's EBITDA (or PAT) ÷ revenue; ₹ revenue targets = the period's total revenue. Delivered when the period is fully reported and the actual is at or above the low end of the guidance (1 point tolerance for growth, 0.5 for margins, 3% for ₹ amounts); Missed when below; Pending while the period is still running (the note shows the figure so far and whether it is on track) or when the metric is not in the results filing (order inflow, loan growth, capex…).

Rules, not judgement: a sentence about one segment ("exports will grow 15–20%") is read as company revenue. Always read the quote.

Searches a compact index of each parsed transcript: up to ~5 KB per call of sentences, management remarks and sentences with numbers or business words first (opening disclaimers and moderator lines removed). Each quarter's index loads only when you search it.

How to read this

From "Schedule of meet" intimations filed with NSE: the first date in the filing's subject line or NSE's summary that falls on or after the filing date. "Earnings call" = the text mentions results, earnings, a quarter or a conference call; the rest are investor conferences, roadshows and one-on-ones.

Dates come from the companies' own text and can be wrong if the filing is ambiguous. Open the filing to confirm the dial-in details.
