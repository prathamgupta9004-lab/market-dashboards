RS Rating (1–99): weighted 12-month price performance (40% last 3 months, 20% each earlier quarter), ranked against every stock scanned, in the style of O'Neil/MarketSmith. Apex Score (1–99): RS Rating 30%, EPS Rating 20%, industry group rank 20%, closeness to the 52-week high 20% and A/D 10%. A/D Rating (A+ to E): turnover on up days versus down days over the last 13 weeks, so A means heavier buying. Leaders = Stage 2 with Apex Score and RS Rating 80+ and EPS Rating 60+. EPS Rating (1–99): year-on-year EPS growth of the last three reported quarters plus acceleration, from NSE XBRL results, ranked the same way. It has no 3–5 year component, so it can differ from MarketSmith's. 90+ solid mint, 70–89 light mint, below 40 red.

Who entered and left Stage 2

How to read this

New = in Stage 2 now, not last week, and not at any point in the 26 weeks before. Re-entry = back in Stage 2 after leaving it within the last 26 weeks. Exit = in Stage 2 last week, now Stage 3 or 4 (or 1), with the weeks it spent in Stage 2 and the return from the close of its first Stage 2 week to this week's close. Continuing = Stage 2 both weeks.

This week compares the current week-to-date bar with last week's final bar. Today compares today's close with yesterday's close, so a stock can flip back before Friday. RS arrows compare the RS Rating now with 2 and 4 weeks ago: ↑↑ 10+ points, ↑ 3–9, → within 2, ↓ and ↓↓ the same down.

The breadth line counts the stocks of the selected list in Stage 2 each week since 2021 (today's members, so delisted stocks are missing). The heatmap shows, for every sector with 5+ stocks in the list, the share of its stocks in Stage 2 over the last 26 weeks.

RS Rating & Apex movers

Whose relative strength is turning

How to read this

RS Rating (1–99) is recomputed for every past week with the same formula as today, so 1W compares the current week with last week's close and 1M with 4 weeks ago. Sparklines show the RS Rating over the last 26 weeks.

RS line at a new high: the stock's price divided by NIFTY 500 (the Mansfield-style RS line) closed above its highest level of the previous 52 weeks. Like MarketSmith's blue dot, it often shows up before the price itself breaks out. The log covers the last 90 days: first and latest day it happened and how many sessions.

Apex movers need stored history: Apex and EPS Ratings are saved every night from the date shown, so 1W/1M changes appear once that much history exists.

How to read this

Pick any week since 2022. The table shows the stocks of the selected list that were in Stage 2 at that week's close, with their weeks in Stage 2, RS Rating and Mansfield RS as they were then, and the return from that close to today next to NIFTY 500's.

What happened in the last 6 sessions

How to read this

Every signal across the selected list over today and the previous 5 sessions, newest first. MA cross = the daily close crossed the 50, 150 or 200-day average (number = distance from it). Turnover spike = traded value at least 3× its 20-session median and ₹1 Cr+ (NSE's daily file has no share volume, so turnover stands in for volume); 1Y high marks the biggest day in a year. Stage change = the weekly stage moved (the current week is provisional until Friday). 52W high = highest close in 52 weeks. ±10% day = adjusted daily move of 10%+. RS line high = price vs NIFTY 500 at a 52-week high.
