Data coverage

Schemes with equity holdings parsed per month from each AMC's official monthly portfolio disclosure. Equity value counts NSE-listed shares only. AUM is each scheme's total net assets, estimated from the disclosed % of NAV.

Method

• Flows = change in shares held × month-end price, adjusted for splits and bonuses (share-count jumps between months). A scheme that didn't file in either month is left out of that month's change, so missing files never show up as exits.
• % of market cap = MF shares ÷ shares outstanding (NSE). Buyers / sellers count schemes that raised or cut their share count by more than 0.5%.
• Fund types come from AMFI scheme categories. Arbitrage funds are hedged, and passive funds track indices, so neither reflects a manager's view; both are excluded from "Active equity".
• Factsheet AMCs (tagged in the table above) don't publish their full monthly portfolio in a form that can be downloaded automatically, so they're built from each scheme's top holdings in the public factsheet. Their value = scheme AUM × % of NAV, and their share counts = value ÷ month-end price. A stock moving into or out of a top list isn't treated as a buy, sell, entry or exit; only stocks listed in both months add to flows.
• Portfolios are as of each month-end and are published by about the 10th of the following month. The page checks daily and picks up a new month automatically.
