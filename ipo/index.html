How to read this

Subscription (×) is shares bid ÷ shares reserved for that category, consolidated across NSE and BSE, from NSE's issue page. QIB = institutions (anchor portion excluded), NII = non-institutional (HNI) investors, split into big NII (bids above ₹10 lakh) and small NII (₹2–10 lakh), Retail = individuals bidding up to ₹2 lakh, Employee = the employee reservation.

The day-by-day chart is built from one snapshot every evening while the issue is open (NSE does not publish a subscription history), so a new issue starts with one point and gains one per bidding day. SME issue pages publish only the overall figure, not every category.

Source: NSE public issues (current, forthcoming, issue information, bid details by category) ·

How to read this

List open / close: listing-day open and close from NSE's daily price file, as % over the issue price. Return: latest close vs the issue price. vs NIFTY 500: the stock's move from its listing-day close to today minus the NIFTY 500's move over the same days (percentage points). Closes only (the vault's daily files carry no intraday highs). Stocks that changed symbol after listing may show no price; stocks that stopped trading show their last close (marked stale).

Source: NSE past issues, NSE issue information, NSE end-of-day prices (vault daily files), NIFTY 500 from NSE indices ·

How to read this

A first base: a recent IPO (listed within ~3 years, at least 30 sessions of trading) that rallied to a post-listing high (the pivot; the last 5 sessions are excluded so a stock breaking out today still shows its old pivot), then pulled back at least 12%, and is now back within X% of that high. Tight = the highest close of the last 15 sessions is at most 12% above the lowest. RS 20d = change in the stock's price relative to the NIFTY 500 over 20 sessions; positive means it is outperforming.

Closing prices only. A screen, not a recommendation.

Source: NSE past issues + NSE end-of-day prices, NIFTY 500 ·
Anchor lock-in expiry calendar

How to read this

Under SEBI's ICDR rules, 50% of the shares allotted to anchor investors are locked in for 30 days and the other 50% for 90 days from the date of allotment. The allotment date is estimated as the trading day before listing (T+3 listing timeline), so a date can be off by a day. Shares = the anchor portion stated on NSE's issue page × 50%; value at the latest close.

Source: NSE issue information (anchor portion), NSE past issues (listing dates), NSE end-of-day prices ·

Recently filed with SEBI

How to read this

Draft offer documents filed with SEBI, newest first. A DRHP is the first draft; a UDRHP is the updated draft filed after SEBI's observations; an addendum amends a draft. SME issues file their drafts with the exchange (NSE Emerge / BSE SME), not SEBI, so they do not appear here.
