How to read this

Each dot is a group plotted against NIFTY 500. Left to right is RS-Ratio: how strong the group's relative trend is (above 100 = beating the index). Bottom to top is RS-Momentum: whether that relative strength is speeding up (above 100) or fading. Both axes are centred on 100.

  • Leading (top-right): outperforming and still gaining.
  • Weakening (bottom-right): still ahead, but the lead is shrinking.
  • Lagging (bottom-left): underperforming and still losing ground.
  • Improving (top-left): still behind, but catching up.

The tail shows the last 8 periods, older points fainter. Groups usually rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging. Tap a dot to highlight its trail. This is an open approximation of the JdK method (z-scored RS over 20 days / 14 weeks), not the licensed RRG® values.

    How to read this

    Every group is ranked by its index return over the chosen window (1 = best). The "ago" columns show the rank the same way, measured one week, one month, three and six months back, so you can see whether a group is climbing or slipping. The trend line plots the weekly rank for the last six months (higher = better rank).

    Reading rule: a group that climbs 15+ places in a month and is now in the top half is gaining leadership, often early in a move. A group that has been top-ranked but slips week after week is losing it. Breadth (% of members above their 50-day average) confirms whether the move is broad or carried by one or two stocks. Tap a row to see its stocks.

    How to read this

    Every tile is a stock, sized by market cap (or 20-day average turnover) and grouped by sector. Mint tiles are up and red tiles are down for the chosen period, and the colour deepens with the size of the move. In RS mode the colour shows the RS rating (99 = strongest, 50 = middle). Each sector header shows its market-cap-weighted move and how many of its stocks rose.

    Tap a tile once for a quick tooltip and tap it again for the stock card. The page address saves your view, so you can share it.

    Each theme is a hand-picked basket of listed stocks that share a story (defence, railways, data centres and so on), tracked as an equal-weight index. Returns show how the basket did; "vs N500" is how far it beat or trailed NIFTY 500 over three months. "> 50 DMA" is the share of members above their 50-day average: a theme rising with most members above it is a broad move, not one stock.

    Tap a theme for its trend chart and member list. Baskets are curated and may miss stocks; they use current members back through history.
