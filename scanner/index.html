How to read this

Pick a scan. Its rules are shown as chips; every number in a chip can be edited and the list updates instantly in your browser. Rules compare today's end-of-day values. "Reset" restores the published defaults, which are also the rules used in Forward Results.

All scans share a universe filter: average daily turnover over 50 sessions of at least the amount below, a minimum price, and no ETFs. RS Rating is a 1–99 percentile of weighted 3/6/9/12-month returns. Stage comes from the Stage 2 scanner. Fundamentals are the latest quarter filed on NSE (point in time: a result counts only from the session after it was filed).

How to read this

Each pattern is detected with the rules listed on its card, using split- and bonus-adjusted daily OHLC from NSE's full bhavcopy. Swing points need 5 bars on each side, so they are confirmed 5 sessions late; nothing uses future prices. The sparkline is the last 90 closes. "Last 5 sessions" counts stocks that showed the pattern on any of the last five days.

    Year
    How to read this

    Each calendar year's 100 biggest runs: the largest gain from a low to a later high inside the year (adjusted closes), among stocks priced ₹20+ with ₹1 Cr+ median daily turnover at the low. Traits are measured at the low, point in time: Weinstein stage and RS Rating (Stage 2 weekly history), latest EPS and sales growth already filed on NSE, up-volume days (up days on above-average volume in the prior 50 sessions), distance from the 52-week high, and the sector's rank by 6-month return (1 = strongest).
