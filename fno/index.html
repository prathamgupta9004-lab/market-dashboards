How to read this

Net index-futures position (long − short contracts) of each participant type, every session since January 2012, from NSE's daily participant-wise open interest files. The lower pane is the FII long ÷ short ratio: above 1 means FIIs hold more long than short index-futures contracts. Pinch or drag to zoom; long-press for the crosshair on touch.

Client = retail and other non-institutional accounts; Pro = brokers' own (proprietary) trading; DII = domestic institutions; FII = foreign portfolio investors. Every long has a short, so the four nets add up to zero.

How to read this

Every F&O stock is one tile. Tile area is its futures open interest value (OI × future price, ₹ Cr) or today's futures turnover (contracts traded × lot × price). Colour is today's price change, OI change, or the build-up class.

Long build-up = price up and OI up (fresh longs). Short build-up = price down, OI up (fresh shorts). Short covering = price up, OI down (shorts exiting). Long unwinding = price down, OI down (longs exiting). Price uses the near-month future; OI is total futures OI across all expiries. Tap a tile for its card.

How to read this

Each bar is one session: how many of the stocks in your current filter fell into each build-up class. A rising red block means shorts are being added across the market; a rising blue block means shorts are being covered. Hover or tap a bar for the counts.

How to read it. Price change uses the near-month future; OI is total futures OI across all expiries, and OI change is NSE's reported change on live contracts, so expiry roll-off does not show as unwinding. 5-day figures chain the daily changes. Rollover = OI in next and far months ÷ total futures OI; previous-series figure is taken on its expiry day. Cost of carry = annualised premium of the nearest future at least 2 days from expiry over spot. Participant data is NSE's participant-wise OI (contracts). Black-76 uses a 6.5% rate.
