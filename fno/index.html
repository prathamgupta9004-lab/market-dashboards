How to read it. Price change uses the near-month future; OI is total futures OI across all expiries, and OI change is NSE's reported change on live contracts, so expiry roll-off does not show as unwinding. 5-day figures chain the daily changes. Rollover = OI in next and far months ÷ total futures OI; previous-series figure is taken on its expiry day. Cost of carry = annualised premium of the nearest future at least 2 days from expiry over spot. Participant data is NSE's participant-wise OI (contracts). Black-76 uses a 6.5% rate.
