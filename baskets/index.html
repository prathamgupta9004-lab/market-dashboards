All variants

How to read thisReturn is since 30 Jan 2026; CAGR annualises that short period, so treat it with care. Volatility is annualised daily volatility. Sharpe and Sortino divide the annualised mean daily return by total and downside volatility, with no risk-free rate. Max drawdown is the worst fall from a peak. Hit rate is the share of fully sold positions closed at a profit. Turnover is the average share of the 25 names replaced at each rebalance. Months beating counts completed months in which the variant beat the index. Tap a row to load that variant.
