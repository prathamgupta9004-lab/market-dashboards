My story

Markets were breakfast-table talk.

I'm Pratham, 24, Head of Research and Principal Officer at Vayu Capital, and previously at 1% Club. I grew up in a Marwari family where markets came up before the chai went cold, and I never really left the table.

Most of my week goes into reading filings, walking exhibition floors and talking to the people who run businesses. These dashboards are the tools I wished existed while doing that, and I share what I learn on YouTube and Instagram.
