Markers
Tap a marker (or any day) to see what happened then.
How to read this

Top pane: closing price adjusted for splits, bonuses and demergers (so old prices are not the ones printed that day). Candles use NSE's full bhavcopy open/high/low, scaled the same way, for the sessions it covers (about the last year). Middle pane: daily traded value (₹ Cr), mint on up days, red on down days. Bottom pane: RS line = stock ÷ NIFTY 500, rescaled so today = 100. A rising RS line means the stock is beating the market.

Markers: R results filing (with next-session reaction), O order win, ▲/▼ insider market buy/sell (all insiders, summed per day), D bulk/block deal by a fund or company (HFT/prop and intraday round-trips excluded), P pledge created/invoked (auto margin pledges excluded), S weekly Weinstein stage change.
