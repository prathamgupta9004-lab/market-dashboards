How to read this

Since added compares today's close with the close on the day you added the stock (stored in your browser). Stage is the Weinstein stage on weekly closes (2 = advancing, 4 = declining) with weeks in that stage. RS is the RS Rating 1–99 (12-month weighted return ranked against all stocks), EPS the EPS Rating 1–99 and APEX the Stage 2 scanner's composite score (RS 30%, EPS 20%, industry group 20%, nearness to 52-week high 20%, A/D 10%). Badges flag ASM/GSM/ESM surveillance, F&O ban, price-band changes, trade-to-trade, recent bulk/block deals, insider filings and results due. The sparkline shows the last 60 sessions.

Click a symbol to open its Stock 360 page. Lists live in this browser only: use Export / backup to move them to another device.
