How to read this
Each row is one NSE filing that fired a trigger, with a one-line headline written by rules (amounts pulled from the filing text). Confidence: H the exchange's own category and the text agree, M matched on the subject or NSE's summary only, L matched only inside the PDF or the text sends mixed signals. ★ marks an order worth 10% or more of the company's trailing-12-month revenue (★★ 25%+). Same company + trigger + day is folded into one row (the count shows as ×N). The green bar marks filings that arrived since your last visit on this device. Tap a row for the detail; "NSE ↗" opens the exchange PDF.

Red flags

Auditor / KMP / independent-director exits, regulator and tax actions, ED/CBI searches, defaults and insolvency, fraud, delayed results, rating downgrades, USFDA warnings, litigation, disruptions and pledge invocations · last 30 days.

How to read this
Sentiment-aware rules: a tax or legal order that the text says was quashed, set aside, dropped or decided in the company's favour is moved to "Favourable order / relief" instead. Retirements, completion of tenure and deaths are not counted as resignations. "% of mcap" sizes a demand or penalty against market value. Always read the filing; small penalties are routine for banks and NBFCs.

Special situations

Buybacks, open offers, delistings, demergers, rights, preferential issues, QIPs, bonus and splits, with prices, ratios and record dates where the filing or NSE's corporate-action list states them.

How to read this
One row per company and event type, combining the filings (last 60 days) with NSE's corporate-action calendar (record / ex dates up to 60 days ahead). Price and ratio are pulled from the text by pattern ("at ₹112 per share", "1:1", "3 for every 8"), so check the filing before acting. "Size" is the issue or buyback amount; "% mcap" compares it to market value.
Source: NSE corporate announcements + NSE corporate actions.

Investor meets

Analyst and institutional-investor meeting intimations: a footprint of which funds are spending time with which managements. Last 30 days.

How to read this
Fund names are read from NSE's summary and the first pages of the intimation PDF, then normalised (e.g. "SBI Funds Management Ltd" → SBI Mutual Fund). Brokers hosting a conference count too. Meeting a company is not buying it, but repeated meetings across a sector show where institutional attention is going. Tap a fund to filter the table.
